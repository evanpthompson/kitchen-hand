HIGH-PROTEIN BUFFALO CHICKEN ENCHILADAS 🍗Stay on top of your health goals with this tasty high-protein chicken recipe. We’re using @farmerfocus chicken because eating well starts with knowing where your food comes from. When you eat Farmer Focus chicken, you can have some peace of mind in knowing where your chicken came from and learning about the farmers responsible for raising it. 👏Get the recipe below! ⬇️
Ingredients:

For the Enchiladas:

2 cooked, shredded Farmer Focus Boneless Skinless Chicken Breasts
4 cups chicken bone broth
1 cup Greek yogurt
1/2 cup buffalo sauce (adjust to taste)
1/2 cup cottage cheese 
1 cup shredded cheddar cheese (divided)
8 tortillas 

For the High-Protein Ranch Dressing:

1/4 cup avocado oil mayonnaise
2 tablespoons dairy-free milk (I like using oat milk) 
1 tablespoon fresh dill
1/2 teaspoon dried parsley
1/2 teaspoon onion powder
1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
1 teaspoon lemon juice
Salt and pepper to taste
