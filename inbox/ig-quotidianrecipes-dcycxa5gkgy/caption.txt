One-Tray Smoked Sausage & Potato Bake
Ingredients 👇🏻
1½ lbs smoked sausage, sliced into ½-inch rounds
2 lbs baby potatoes, halved or quartered
3 tablespoons olive oil
4 cloves garlic, minced
1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
1 teaspoon dried parsley
½ teaspoon smoked paprika
½ teaspoon onion powder
½ teaspoon salt, or to taste
¼ teaspoon black pepper
¼ teaspoon red pepper flakes, optional
⅓ cup grated Parmesan cheese
2 tablespoons fresh parsley, chopped, optional

Instructions 👇🏻
Preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C). Lightly grease a large rimmed baking sheet or a 9x13-inch baking dish.
Add the potatoes to a large bowl. Toss with olive oil, garlic, Italian seasoning, dried parsley, smoked paprika, onion powder, salt, black pepper, and red pepper flakes.
Spread the potatoes out in a single layer and roast for 20 minutes so they get a head start on becoming tender.
Remove the tray from the oven, add the sliced smoked sausage, and toss everything together.
Return the tray to the oven and bake for another 20–25 minutes, stirring once halfway through, until the potatoes are fork-tender and the sausage is browned around the edges.
Sprinkle the Parmesan over the top and bake for another 3–5 minutes, just until it melts slightly and clings to the potatoes and sausage.
Finish with fresh parsley if you like, then serve hot. It pairs well with a simple salad, roasted vegetables, or warm bread.
Store leftovers in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 4 days. Reheat in the oven or air fryer for the best texture.

Prep Time: 15 minutes | Cook Time: 45 minutes | Total Time: 1 hour
Servings: 6 | Calories: approximately 520 per serving
