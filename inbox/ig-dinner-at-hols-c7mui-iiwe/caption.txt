Garlic butter chicken Mac n Cheese
.
⬇️ Recipe below ⬇️ 
.
Recipe serves 4 
.
Press save for dinner inspiration 📌 follow @dinner_at_hols for more recipes like this 
.
Ingredients. 
.
300g pasta (uncooked weight) 
500g chicken breast 
1tbs chicken seasoning- I used @justspices_uk
1tbs garlic granules 
1/2tbs parsley 
2tbs butter 
4 cloves garlic (finely grated) 
2tbs plain flour 
200ml hot chicken stock
300ml milk, warmed 
1tsp mustard - I used English mustard (optional) 
120g cheese- I used a mix of extra mature cheddar, and mozzarella. 
Season to taste
Parsley to garnish (optional) 
.
Method.
.
Start by seasoning the chicken. Fry the chicken until it’s cooked through and browned then add in the butter and garlic. Cook for a few minutes and then remove the chicken from the pan and set aside. Add in the flour to the butter and give it a good mix, then add in the stock and milk slowly, bit by bit stirring continuously until you have a thick sauce. Add in the mustard (optional) and the cheese and mix until the cheese has melted, then add in the cooked pasta and season to taste. Add some pasta water if needed. Add the chicken back in. Garnish, Serve and enjoy. 
.
