#sponsored Steakhouse Cheeseburger Mac is an easy one pan dinner the whole family enjoys! @texasroadhouse 

INGREDIENTS 
1 lb ground beef 
1 onion, chopped 
1/2 tsp ground mustard 
1 tsp each of onion powder, garlic powder, and smoked paprika, and steak seasoning 
1 tbsp minced garlic 
1/4 cup Texas Roadhouse Classic Steak Sauce
1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce 
2 1/2 cups elbow macaroni
3 cups of beef broth 
8 oz shredded Colby Jack cheese 
1/3 cup of milk
Dried parsley 

INSTRUCTIONS 
Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat. Brown 1 lb of ground beef with chopped onion. Season with ground mustard, onion powder, garlic powder, smoked paprika, and some steak seasoning. 

Once the beef is browned, drain any extra grease from the meat. 

Next, add the minced garlic, Texas Roadhouse Steak sauce, and Worcestershire sauce. Stir well. Add the uncooked pasta and beef broth to the skillet. 

Bring to a quick boil then turn the heat down to medium-low. Cover with a lid and cook for about 10-15 minutes stirring once or twice to keep the pasta from sticking to the bottom of the skillet. 

Once the pasta is cooked add the shredded cheese and milk. Stir everything together well and top with dried parsley. Enjoy! Let me know if you try it out!
