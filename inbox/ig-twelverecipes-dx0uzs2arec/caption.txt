Creamy Garlic Chicken and Cheesy Rice Skillet Delight

Ingredients:

2 cups cooked white rice
1 lb chicken breast (cut into bite-sized cubes)
1 1/2 cups heavy cream
1 cup shredded mozzarella cheese
1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
3 cloves garlic (minced)
2 tbsp butter
1 tbsp olive oil
1 tsp Italian seasoning
1/2 tsp paprika
Salt and pepper to taste
Fresh parsley (chopped)

Step-by-step:

Heat olive oil in a pan and cook chicken pieces until golden and fully cooked.
Season with paprika, salt, pepper, and Italian seasoning.
Add butter and garlic, sauté for 1–2 minutes until fragrant.
Pour in heavy cream and let it simmer for 2–3 minutes.
Stir in mozzarella and Parmesan cheese until melted and creamy.
Add cooked rice and mix well until fully coated.
Cook for another 2–3 minutes until heated through.
Garnish with parsley and serve hot.

Cooking Time: 30 minutes
Calories: ~620 kcal per serving
