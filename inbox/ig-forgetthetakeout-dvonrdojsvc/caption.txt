the classic flavors of a McDonald’s McGriddle made into a higher protein McGriddle breakfast cake 
✨recipe below✨I shared these in muffin form a while back and y’all loved them! 

Ingredients 
1 cup flour (sub gluten-free)
1 tablespoon baking powder 
2 eggs
1/2 cup cottage cheese 
1/3 cup maple syrup
3/4 cup milk
1/2 lb ground breakfast sausage
1 cup shredded cheddar cheese

Maple syrup, for serving

Ingredients 
1. Preheat oven to 375 degrees. Grease an 8”x 8” baking dish, line with parchment paper. 
2. Heat a large skillet, add sausage and cook until browned, breaking into small pieces. Set aside.
3. In a large mixing bowl combine flour, baking powder, eggs, cottage cheese, milk and maple syrup. Mix well, then fold in sausage and cheese.
4. Pour batter into baking dish and spread evenly. 
Bake in the oven for 35-40 minutes, or until slightly browned and when a toothpick is inserted it comes out clean. 
5. Slice and serve with extra maple syrup.
