Easy Roasted Garlic Butter Chicken by @simple.home.edit
 
Welcome back to 30 days of family dinners on a budget, the series where I share with you easy, family dinner ideas that won’t have you spending hours in the kitchen. This is easy roasted garlic butter chicken made using economical and easy to cook chicken thigh cutlets, my family loved this recipe. The chicken is coated in a garlic butter marinade and then is popped in the oven with a little chicken stock which keeps the chicken tender and creates the most delicious sauce. It’s comforting and low fuss with minimal washing up. Try this one, you will love it!
