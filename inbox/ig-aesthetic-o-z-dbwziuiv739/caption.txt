My Low-Calorie, High-Protein Garlic Cheesesticks (16 sticks)

~123 Calories | 12g Protein Per Stick

Crust Ingredients:
• 450g fat-free cottage cheese
• 3 large eggs
• 170g self-rising flour
• 40g fat-free shredded cheese
• 40g 2% shredded cheese
• Salt, garlic powder, seasonings to taste

Crust Instructions:

1. Blend cottage cheese + eggs until smooth
2. Pour into bowl and mix in flour + seasonings
3. Line baking tray with parchment paper and lightly spray with oil
4. Spread mixture evenly onto tray
5. Bake at 400°F for 20 minutes

Toppings:
• 4 servings light butter
• 15 g minced garlic
• 280g cheese total (half fat-free, half 2%)

Final Bake:

1. Remove crust from oven
2. Mix together minced garlic, and butter, then melt (15-30 sec in microwave)
3. Pour on top of crust then add cheese on top
4. Bake again at 400°F for 5–10 mins until cheese melts and crust browns to your liking

Slice into 16 pieces & enjoy.
