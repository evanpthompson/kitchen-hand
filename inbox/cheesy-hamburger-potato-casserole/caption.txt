Cheesy Hamburger Potato Casserole
A hearty comfort-food casserole layered with seasoned ground beef, tender potatoes, creamy sauce, and plenty of melted cheese.

Ingredients
450 g ground beef
900 g potatoes, thinly sliced
1 small onion, diced
2 garlic cloves, minced
1 tbsp butter
1 can cream of mushroom soup
¾ cup milk
1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
1 tsp garlic powder
½ tsp paprika
½ tsp dried thyme
¾ tsp salt
½ tsp black pepper
2 cups shredded cheddar cheese, divided
1 tbsp chopped parsley, optional

Instructions
Preheat the oven to 190°C. Lightly grease a 23 × 33 cm baking dish.
Wash and peel the potatoes if desired. Slice them into thin, even rounds, about 3–4 mm thick.
Dice the onion and mince the garlic.
Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the ground beef and onion and cook for 6–8 minutes, breaking the beef apart until browned.
Drain excess grease. Add the garlic, Worcestershire sauce, paprika, garlic powder, thyme, salt, and pepper. Cook for 1 minute.
In a bowl, whisk the cream of mushroom soup and milk until smooth.
Arrange half the sliced potatoes across the bottom of the baking dish, slightly overlapping.
Spread half the beef mixture over the potatoes. Spoon over half the creamy sauce and sprinkle with ½ cup cheddar.
Repeat with the remaining potatoes, beef, and sauce.
Cover tightly with foil and bake for 50–55 minutes, until the potatoes are nearly tender.
Remove the foil and sprinkle the remaining cheese evenly over the top.
Bake uncovered for another 15–20 minutes, until the potatoes are fork-tender and the cheese is melted and golden.
Let the casserole rest for 10 minutes before serving. Sprinkle with parsley if desired. Refrigerate leftovers for up to 3 days.

Prep Time: 20 minutes | Cook Time: 1 hour 15 minutes | Total Time: 1 hour 35 minutes
Servings: 6 | Calories: Approximately 520 per serving
