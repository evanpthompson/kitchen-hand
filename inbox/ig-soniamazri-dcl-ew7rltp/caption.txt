Day 21 of 30 Family Dinners Toddlers Actually Eat 🤍

One thing that completely changed dinner time for us was realizing I didn’t need to make a separate meal for my toddler. We all eat the same dinner, with simple age-appropriate modifications, and it’s been such a lifesaver.

Tonight’s dinner: Dump & Bake Cheddar Broccoli Chicken Orzo 🥦🧀🍗

Why this meal works:

• Chicken thighs → provide protein, iron, and healthy fats to support growing little bodies.
• Broccoli → packed with fiber, vitamin C, and antioxidants to support healthy digestion and immunity.
• Cheddar cheese → provides calcium, protein, and healthy fats for strong bones and muscles.
• Orzo → provides carbohydrates for lasting energy to keep little ones fueled throughout the day.

Everything bakes together in one dish, so there’s minimal prep, fewer dishes, and one delicious dinner the whole family can enjoy—including your toddler. 🤍
