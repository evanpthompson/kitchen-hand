Meal Prep Honey BBQ Chicken 🍗 

Calories 184 
Protein 22.5g Fats 2.8g Carbs 8.3g 

6 portions 

600g chicken breast 
250g tomato passata
50g bbq sauce 
30g honey 
Hot sauce to your liking 
Any seasoning - I threw in taco seasoning, chilli, garlic, pepper 

cook on high for 4 hours or 7-8 low and shred and you’re done! 

I leave it all in the fridge and throughout the week add to buns, salads - whatever you wish 🤝🏼
