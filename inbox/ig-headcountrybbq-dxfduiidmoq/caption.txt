Make some Bbq on a budget with these Poor Man’s Burnt Ends!🔥

Ingredients:

1 Chuck Roast
1 bottle Head Country Original BBQ Sauce
Brown sugar
Half stick butter
Brisket or SPG Rub

Directions:

Start off by setting to cook over indirect heat at around 250F - 275F degrees. 

Then go ahead and season up your chuck roast generously. Let it sit for about 15 minutes and get it on to smoke. 

Once you’ve got the color and bark you’re looking for, go ahead and cube it up and throw it in a pan with the bbq sauce, brown sugar, and butter. Wrap with foil. 

Then throw it back on to cook until the cubes reach an internal of 205F-210F degrees and you’re ready to eat.
