Bacon cheeseburger rice skillet 
-1 lb ground beef
-2 tbsp butter
-1/2 diced up sweet onion
-1 tbsp minced garlic
-2 cups instant rice
-2 cups beef broth
-1 8 oz can tomato sauce
-3 tbsp ketchup 
-2 tbsp mustard
-1 1/2 tsp of burger seasoning
-1 tsp all purpose seasoning
-1/2 tsp onion powder
-1/2 tsp garlic powder
-black pepper to taste
-1/4 cup real bacon bits (plus some for topping)
-1/2 cup cheese (plus some for topping)

Add your butter to a hot skillet and let it melt. Then add in your onions and sauté for a few minutes until slightly translucent. Then add in your ground beef and cook that until it’s done. Add in your mixed garlic and instant rice and let that toast for a couple of minutes while you stir. Then add in your broth, tomato sauce, ketchup, mustard, and seasonings. Combine everything let that come to a boil cut the heat, cover it, and let it sit for 5 to 7 minutes until your rice is done. Fluff your rice up and add in your bacon bits and cheese. Stir it to melt your cheese and then top with a little more shredded cheese and bacon bits. It’s ready to serve !
