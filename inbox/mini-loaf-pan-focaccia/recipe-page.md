Yield: 1 loaf

Ingredients
- 120 grams all-purpose flour (1 cup, aerated and loosely scooped, plus more for dusting)
- 5 grams Diamond Crystal Kosher Salt (1 teaspoon + a heaping 1/2 teaspoon)
- 5 grams sugar (1 1/4 teaspoons)
- 3 grams instant yeast (1 teaspoon)
- 10 grams extra virgin olive oil (2 1/2 teaspoons)
- 90 grams warm water 90F (1/3 cup + 2 teaspoons)
- 6-12 grams additional oil for the pan and drizzling on top (1 1/2-3 teaspoons)
- Additional salt and/or herbs for topping

Instructions
1. Mix: Whisk together dry ingredients in a medium bowl. Make a well in centre, add warm water and olive oil. Stir liquid, slowly incorporating flour from bowl sides until sticky dough forms. Fold dough over itself, rotating bowl until lumpy and cohesive with no dry flour remaining. Gather in messy ball. Cover and rest 15 minutes.
2. First fold: Perform slap-and-fold in bowl with damp hand 4-6 times until dough tightens slightly. Cover and rest 15 minutes.
3. Second fold: Repeat slap-and-fold process until dough tightens. Cover and rest 15 minutes.
4. Pre-shape and rise: Spray loaf pan with cooking spray, drizzle with approximately 1 tablespoon olive oil. Transfer dough to pan, gently stretch into loose rectangle. Cover and rise at room temperature until doubled, about 1 hour.
5. Dimple and top: Drizzle top with olive oil, dimple with oiled fingers. Cover and rise 15-20 minutes until dimples fill slightly.
6. Preheat: During final 30 minutes of rise, preheat oven to 400F with rack in centre position.
7. Bake: Bake until golden brown on top, 20-25 minutes.
8. Cool: Turn out onto wire rack immediately. Cool at least 10 minutes before slicing.
