This is a variation of a dish sometimes called “company potatoes,” popular in the postwar kitchens of the 1950s, made with canned condensed soups and frozen hash browns. Maura Passanisi, of Alameda, California, shared it with @nytimes last year as a tribute to her grandmother, Florence Sweeney, who originally served it as a #Thanksgiving side dish. Maura uses fresh russet #potatoes and no condensed soup in her version, but plenty of cream cheese, sour cream, butter and cheese. “Legendary,” she called the dish. And so it is.
