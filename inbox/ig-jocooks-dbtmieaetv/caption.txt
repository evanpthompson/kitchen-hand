Mongolian Beef Ramen. Tender flank steak, sweet savory sauce, ramen noodles that soak up every last drop. 30 minutes. One skillet. Better than takeout and it's not even close.

Two things that make the difference:
🥩 Freeze the steak for 20 minutes before slicing. You get clean, thin, even cuts every time.
🍜 Cook the noodles just until tender. They finish in the sauce and if they go in overcooked they go mushy fast.

That's it. The rest is just the sauce doing what it was born to do.
