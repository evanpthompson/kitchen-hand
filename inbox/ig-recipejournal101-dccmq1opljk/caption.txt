Instructions for All Marinades:
Add all marinade ingredients to a large zip-top bag and mix well. Add the beef, seal the bag, and massage until evenly coated. Refrigerate for 2–8 hours (Bulgogi: 30 minutes–4 hours). Remove beef from the marinade and discard the used marinade. Grill, pan-sear, or stir-fry until cooked to your preferred doneness. Let whole steaks rest for 5–10 minutes before slicing against the grain.

Freezer Meal Prep:
For any of these, place the raw beef and marinade together in a freezer-safe zip-top bag, squeeze out excess air, flatten, label, and freeze for up to 3 months. Thaw overnight in the refrigerator—the beef will marinate as it thaws. Discard marinade that has been in contact with raw beef unless you boil it thoroughly before using it as a sauce.
