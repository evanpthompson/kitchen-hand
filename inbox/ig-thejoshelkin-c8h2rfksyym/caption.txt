The Perfect Breakfast Potatoes, Everytime! 
- Small dice some yukon gold potatoes
- Add them to a pot of salted water and boil for 10 minutes
- Drain the potatoes
- Add some olive oil and butter to a medium high frying pan
- Add the potatoes and toss in the olive oil and butter
- Cook them without stirring for roughly 3 minutes
- Toss them and add some onion powder, smoked paprika, garlic powder and salt
- Cook stirring frequently for roughly 3 more minutes
- Mix in some chopped parsley
- Eat them on their own or with a side of bacon and eggs
