Creamy Garlic Parmesan Rigatoni with Juicy Steak Bites

Ingredients:

2 cups rigatoni pasta
1 lb steak (sirloin or ribeye, cubed)
1 cup heavy cream
3/4 cup parmesan cheese (grated)
2 tbsp butter
1 tbsp olive oil
3 cloves garlic (minced)
1 tsp Italian seasoning
1/2 tsp paprika
Salt & pepper to taste
Fresh parsley (chopped)

Step-by-step:

Cook rigatoni according to package instructions, then drain.
Season steak cubes with salt, pepper, paprika, and Italian seasoning.
Heat olive oil in a pan and sear steak bites until browned and juicy, then set aside.
In the same pan, melt butter and sauté garlic until fragrant.
Add heavy cream and let it simmer for 2–3 minutes.
Stir in parmesan cheese until the sauce becomes smooth and creamy.
Toss the cooked rigatoni in the sauce until fully coated.
Plate the creamy pasta and add steak bites on the side.
Garnish with parsley and extra parmesan.

Cooking Time: 30 minutes
Calories: ~950 kcal per serving
