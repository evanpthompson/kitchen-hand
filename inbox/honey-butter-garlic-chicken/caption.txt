Easy & Healthy Honey Butter Garlic Chicken🍗🍯
High Protein Meal Prep 💪🏼

(Macros: Per Serve - 4 Total / Including Rice)
539 Calories
70gC | 7gF | 47gP

(Macros: Per Serve - 4 Total / Chicken ONLY)
318 Calories
23gC | 6gF | 42gP

Ingredients:
700g Diced Skinless & Boneless Chicken Breast (raw weight)
30ml Soy Sauce (Kikkoman Salt Reduced Soy Sauce)
1 Tsp White & Black Pepper
5g Minced Garlic & 5g Minced Ginger
100g Potato Starch (not all of this gets used - approximately 1/2 sticks to the chicken in total)
35g Light Butter (Nuttelex Light)
4 Diced Garlic Cloves
75ml Soy Sauce & 75ml Water
30g Brown Sugar Stevia (Natvia Gold)
Red Chilli Flakes (optional)
1 Tsp Cornstarch Mixed With Cold Water (helps thicken the sauce)
45g Honey (Capilano Hot Chilli Honey - or your choice of honey)
700g Cooked White Rice (175g per serving / 4 total - amount can be adjusted to your liking)

Don’t forget to check out my digital cook books with over 200 easy & delicious recipes like these! 📖👨🏻‍🍳

Important Notes:
Make sure to spray the chicken evenly with light cooking oil spray after coating - potato starch is the key to making the chicken extra crispy when air frying. Flip the chicken once it’s half way cooked & give it another spray on any dry areas.
Use reduced salt soy sauce to ensure the sauce isn’t too salty - taste & adjust if needed to suit your liking.
