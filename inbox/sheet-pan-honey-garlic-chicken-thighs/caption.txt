SHEET PAN HONEY GARLIC CHICKEN THIGHS✨ a simple and super flavorful way to jazz up your weeknight chicken! We’re going to flash-marinate the chicken while it comes to room temperature, roast it on a parchment-lined baking sheet (because weeknights are for mess-free cooking 😉), and baste it in our delicious honey garlic sauce! I should also mention that this would be a great way to make your meal prep chicken a little more *exciting* 😜

Ingredients: 
1 1/2 lbs chicken thighs
1/4 cup avocado oil
1/4 cup honey 
2 tbsp tamari
4 cloves garlic, minced 
1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
1 1/2 tsp paprika 
1 tsp onion powder 
For serving: prepared rice, sliced green onions, fresh lime juice, chopped fresh cilantro

Directions: 
Combine the avocado oil, honey, tamari, garlic, apple cider vinegar, paprika, and onion powder in a small glass or bowl and whisk to combine 
Take the chicken thighs out of the fridge and add them to a bowl with a lid (or a ziplock bag)
Pour the sauce over top, seal, and allow them to marinate while they come to room temperature before cooking (20-30 minutes) 
Preheat your oven to 400
Remove the chicken from the marinade, saving the marinade for later, place the chicken thighs on a parchment-lined baking sheet and roast for 25 minutes
While the chicken is cooking, add the marinade to a small saucepan and bring to a boil
Reduce to a simmer and simmer for 5-7 minutes until thickened, stirring often 
Once the chicken has cooked and your sauce has simmered, serve the chicken over prepared rice and baste generously with the sauce 
Enjoy!
