The Ultimate Cheesy Crispy Hashbrown Beef Casserole!

Description:
You can hear that crunch as we dig in! This cheesy beef casserole is the ultimate comfort food—perfectly creamy on the inside and irresistibly crispy on top.

Ingredients:

1 lb ground beef

1 small onion, diced

2 cloves garlic, minced

1 can (10.5 oz) cream of mushroom soup

1 cup sour cream

2 cups shredded cheddar cheese, divided

1 package (30 oz) frozen shredded hashbrowns, thawed

4 tbsp melted butter

Salt, pepper, and garlic powder to taste

Instructions:

Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C) and grease a 9x13-inch baking dish.

In a skillet, brown the ground beef with the diced onion and garlic over medium-high heat. Drain excess grease and season with salt, pepper, and garlic powder.

In a large bowl, mix the cream of mushroom soup, sour cream, and 1.5 cups of the cheddar cheese. Stir in the cooked ground beef.

Gently fold in the thawed hashbrowns and melted butter until well combined.

Spread the mixture evenly into the prepared baking dish. Top with the remaining 1/2 cup of cheddar cheese.

Bake for 35-40 minutes until the top is golden brown and beautifully crispy. Let cool slightly before serving.
