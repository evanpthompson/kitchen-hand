Maple Sausage Butter Swim Biscuit Recipe- an easy breakfast/brunch recipe! 🍁🧀

Are you into sweet & savory?

Recipe found at https://www.ameliaisnotachef.com/recipes/maple-sausage-butter-swim-biscuits

INGREDIENTS
* ½ cup (1 stick) salted butter, sliced (for the pan)
* 2½ cups all-purpose flour
* 4 teaspoons baking powder
* 1½ teaspoons kosher salt
* ¼ cup granulated sugar
* ½ teaspoon black pepper
* 1 1/2 cups breakfast sausage, cooked, crumbled, and cooled
* 1 cup shredded sharp cheddar cheese (freshly shredded is best)
* 2 cups buttermilk, room temperature
* 2 tablespoons melted butter, slightly cooled (for the biscuit batter)
* 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
* Maple Butter Glaze
* 2 tablespoons salted butter, melted
* 3 tablespoons pure maple syrup
* Maldon salt for topping (optional)

INSTRUCTIONS
1. Preheat the oven to 450°F. Place the sliced butter in a 9x9-inch glass baking dish and place it in the oven until melted (about 5-8 minutes). Carefully remove the pan from the oven.
2. In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, salt, sugar, and black pepper.
3. Add the cooked sausage and shredded cheddar to the flour mixture and toss until evenly coated.
4. In a large measuring cup, whisk together the buttermilk, melted butter, and vanilla extract.
5. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and stir just until combined. Do not overmix.
6. Spread the batter evenly over the melted butter in the baking dish. Using a butter knife, score the dough into 9 squares.
7. Bake for 25-35 minutes, or until the center is cooked through and the top is golden brown. NOTE: Place lined pan underneath to catch potential spillage NOTE 2: Keep your eye out for browning- I tented loosely with foil around 24 minutes and then baked another 10 minutes until center was cooked through- times will vary depending on your oven.
8. While the biscuits bake, whisk together the melted butter and maple syrup
9. Brush the maple butter glaze generously over the hot biscuits immediately after removing them from the oven and sprinkle with maldon salt. Let cool for 5–10minutes before serving.
