Italian Chicken & Rice in the ole Crock Pot. Tastes like pure magic, slow cooker szn is back.
(420 cals, 45g protein, 24g carbs, 15g fat) – 10 large servings

👉 Cook on high 4 hrs, shred chicken, add cooked rice, let cook 30 more mins to soak up all that flavor. I used five cups of cooked jasmine but you can do as much as your heart desires. 
Grease the cheeks. Love you 🖤

Ingredients
	•	2 lbs boneless skinless chicken thighs
	•	2 lbs boneless skinless chicken breasts
	•	2 cups chicken bone broth
	•	1 block cream cheese
	•	½ cup grated parm
	•	1 tbsp Italian seasoning
	•	1 tsp garlic powder
	•	1 tbsp crushed red pepper
	•	½ cup Olive Garden Italian dressing
