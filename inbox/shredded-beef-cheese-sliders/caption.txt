SHREDDED BEEF AND CHEESE SLIDERS! 😋
 
Ingredients:
-King’s Hawaiian Original Hawaiian Sweet Rolls
-1 4-5 pound chuck roast
-2 tablespoons olive oil
-3 tablespoons salt, pepper and garlic blend seasoning
-2 onions (sliced)
-6 slices Swiss cheese
-1/2 cup Mayo
-2 tablespoons Worcestershire sauce
-1 tablespoon fresh thyme
-2 cups beef broth 
 
Preparation:
1. Coat chuck roast with olive oil, salt, pepper, and garlic powder
2. Smoke chuck roast at 250 degrees until it reaches an internal temperature of 175 degrees
3. Place chuck roast into a roasting pan with sliced onions, beef broth, Worcestershire sauce, and season with additional salt, pepper, and garlic seasoning. Then, cover with aluminum foil and bake at 350 degrees until the beef is fork tender and can be shredded apart
4. Spread garlic mayo (mayo, garlic paste, Worcestershire sauce, fresh thyme) to the bottom of King’s Hawaiian Original Hawaiian Sweet Rolls, then add shredded beef and Swiss cheese. Place top buns on top and bake at 350 for 15 minutes
