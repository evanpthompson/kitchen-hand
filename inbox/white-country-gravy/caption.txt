Fried Chicken & Country Gravy 🍗 Easy 30 minute dinner idea! Easy way to bring back home-cooked meals 🍽️. **Gravy Recipe below 👇
 
White Country Gravy Recipe:
Ingredients:
2 Tbsp butter
3 Tbsp grease drippings (or more butter)
½ cup all purpose flour
4 cups milk
Salt
Black pepper

Instructions:
1. In a large skillet, heat the butter and grease over medium heat. Once melted, add the flour and stir for 1-2 minutes. 
2. Once fully combined and slightly brown, slowly pour in the milk while stirring. 
3.  Continue to stir occasionally until desired thickness. Takes 5-10 minutes.
4. Season to taste with salt and pepper.
