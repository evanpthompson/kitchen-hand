Crockpot Sausage and Potato Casserole
This Crockpot Sausage and Potato Casserole is a hearty, cheesy comfort dish packed with smoky sausage, tender potatoes, and melted cheese. It’s an easy slow cooker meal perfect for busy days and family dinners.

Ingredients:
1 1/2 pounds smoked sausage, sliced
2 pounds potatoes, diced into bite-sized pieces
1 small onion, diced
2 cups shredded cheddar cheese
1 cup heavy cream
1/2 cup chicken broth
2 tablespoons butter, melted
1 teaspoon garlic powder
1 teaspoon onion powder
1 teaspoon paprika
1/2 teaspoon salt
1/2 teaspoon black pepper
1 teaspoon dried parsley (optional)

Instructions:
Lightly grease the crockpot and add diced potatoes and onion.
Layer sliced sausage evenly over the potatoes.
In a bowl, mix heavy cream, chicken broth, melted butter, garlic powder, onion powder, paprika, salt, and pepper.
Pour the mixture evenly over the sausage and potatoes.
Cover and cook on low for 6 to 7 hours or high for 3 to 4 hours, until potatoes are tender.
Sprinkle shredded cheddar cheese over the top during the last 20 minutes of cooking.
Cover again and let the cheese melt completely.
Garnish with parsley if desired and serve warm. Store leftovers in the fridge for up to 3 days.

Prep Time: 15 minutes | Cook Time: 6 hours | Total Time: 6 hours 15 minutes
Servings: 6 | Calories: 520 per serving
