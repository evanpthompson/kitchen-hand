High Protein Creamy Garlic Steak Pasta! 🔥🥩
By @panaceapalm 👈

ONLY 500 Calories! Easy Meal Prep 😋

Serves 4: 🍽️🍽️🍽️🍽️

Calories & Macros 📊
Per Meal: 532 calories
54g P | 57g C | 9g F

Seasoned Steak Bites:
- 700g diced lean beef steaks (Topside or flank steak are the leanest cuts)
- Tsp garlic powder
- Tsp onion powder
- Tsp salt
- Tsp black pepper
- Tsp parsley
- Cooking oil spray

Creamy Garlic Sauce
- 250ml low fat (1%) milk
- 150g light cream cheese (brand: Philadelphia lightest)
- 2-3 garlic cloves
- Tsp salt
- Tsp black pepper
- Tsp parsley

Other ingredients:
- 520g cooked penne pasta (250g uncooked weight)
- 1/2 medium white onion (diced)
- 2 garlic cloves (minced)
- Tsp paprika
- Chopped chives (optional garnish)
