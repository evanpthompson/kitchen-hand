Honey BBQ Chicken tenders. These were so good! Recipes ⤵️

Cooking Instructions: 
* Season your chicken with your favorite rub 
* Drop it in marinade (below) for 30 minutes up to overnight 
* Remove from marinade and cover in flour dredge (below). Make sure all parts are covered 
* Fry at 350° for 5-6 minutes, until golden brown 
* Coat your chicken in @fyrstarter ‘s new Honey FYR sauce 
* Enjoy! 
Marinade:
* 2 cups buttermilk
* 1 Tbsp salt
* ½ Tbsp garlic powder
* 2 Tbsp hot sauce
* 1 tsp paprika
Flour Dredge: 
* 3 cups flour
* ⅓ cup potato starch (sub cornstarch)
* 2 tsp garlic
*2 tsp paprika 
* 2 tsp salt
* 2 tsp black pepper
