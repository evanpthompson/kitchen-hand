Forgotten Chicken Casserole 
There’s no forgetting this chicken dish as it goes way to my mom’s college days. She said they always made it because it was super simple and so delicious. We love making it today still especially when we need something that’s easy that everyone likes. The prep time is ridiculously quick and your oven does all of the work. There’s no reason not to cook when a dinner can be this simple.

6-8 chicken thighs can use boneless or bone in or breasts 
1 1/2 cups white long grain rice 
1 can cream of mushroom soup 
1 can cream of chicken soup 
1 packet onion soup mix 
2 cups water
Salt and pepper 
Paprika 

Add the rice to a prepared 9 x13 baking dish. Mix the soups, onion soup mix and water. Pour over rice. Nestle the chicken over top. Sprinkle with salt, pepper and paprika. Cover and bake at 350 for 1 1/2 -2 hours.
