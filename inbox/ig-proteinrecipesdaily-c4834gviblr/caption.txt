HIGH PROTEIN, LOW CALORIE Sweet Potato Breakfast Meal Prep Bowls 🍠 🍳 🤤By @doosifit 

Macros (per serving/bowl) ⬇️
381 calories
44g protein
22g carb
13g fat

Prep time: 15 minutes
Cook time: 30 minutes
Servings: 6 bowls (video only shows 3 servings)

Ingredients for 6 servings/bowls ⬇️

Turkey sausage (36 mini discs) ⬇️
2 lb ground turkey
2 tsp salt
2 tsp garlic powder
2 tsp paprika
1 tsp black pepper
2 tbsp lite syrup
1 tsp olive oil

Sweet potatoes ⬇️
4 sweet potatoes, peeled and chopped (~600g without skin)
1/2 tbsp olive oil
2 tsp salt
2 tsp paprika
1 tsp garlic powder
➡️ bake 400 degrees, 30 minutes (monitor closely, don’t overcrowd tray, do multiple trays if necessary)

Eggs ⬇️
736g egg beaters 
1/2 tsp salt
1/2 tsp black pepper
