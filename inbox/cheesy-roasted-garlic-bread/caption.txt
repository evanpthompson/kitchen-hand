Cheesy roasted garlic bread🥖 

Ingredients 

1/2 cup softened unsalted butter ( 1 stick )
8-10 garlic cloves 
1/2 cup olive oil 
1 teaspoons red chili flakes 
Salt to taste
1/2 cup fresh mozzarella cheese
1 baguette 
1/4 cup fresh chopped parsley 

Method: Instructions:

Heat the oven to 400 F. In an oven safe bowl add garlic cloves and half cup olive oil. Bake for 30-35 minutes or until the garlic is soft.

In another bowl add butter, baked garlic cloves, red chili flakes , salt, and parsley. Mix well until nice and smooth.

Slice baguette in half lengthwise, spread good amount of garlic mixture onto both sides , top it with mozzarella cheese. Then bake both halves until toasty for about 15-20 minutes! Garnish with parsley.
