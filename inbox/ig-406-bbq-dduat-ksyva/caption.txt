Crockpot Chuck Roast Kalbi 🔥🐄

Welcome to my Crockpot Meals series, where I help you make easy recipes so good, you might just find a spouse—or impress the one you’ve already got. 😘

Kalbi is usually made with short ribs, but those can get expensive. So I used an affordable chuck roast from @costco and gave it those same sweet, savory, garlicky flavors, and let the @crockpot turn it fall-apart tender.

Ingredients:

2 lbs chuck roast
Your favorite beef seasoning
1 cup shoyu
1 cup brown sugar
2 tablespoon sesame oil
2 tablespoons rice vinegar
2 tablespoons Thai chili
2 tablespoons grated ginger
2 tablespoons minced garlic 1/4 cup of water
1 cup grated Asian pear
Favorite seasoning

Instructions:

1. Pat the chuck roast dry and season it on every side. Go light on the salt because the shoyu will bring plenty.
2. Heat your frying pan over medium-high heat and add 2T butter. Sear the roast on all sides until you build a beautiful crust. We aren’t cooking it to temperature—we’re just building flavor.
3. Mix the shoyu, brown sugar, sesame oil, rice vinegar, Thai chili, ginger, garlic, and grated Asian pear.
4. Place the seared chuck roast in your crockpot and pour the kalbi mixture over it.
5. Cover and cook on LOW for 4–6 hours, or until the meat pulls apart easily. A thicker roast may need a little longer, so let tenderness—not just the clock—tell you when it’s ready.

This was seriously delicious and couldn’t have been easier to make. The crockpot did the work. You take the credit 🤙🏽
